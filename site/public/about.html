(01) — Who we are

Character,
not mass production.

RETROMOD. is where design-led pieces from the mid-century and beyond find their next home — from Danish teak and Italian lighting to Japanese amplifiers and cameras.

Everything on our floor is hand-selected. Each piece is photographed and described honestly, with its maker, era and origin, before it goes live on Instagram — where our community of collectors, designers and Hi-Fi lovers usually snaps it up within days.

We're buyers as well as sellers. If you have a piece that deserves a second life, we'd love to see it.
