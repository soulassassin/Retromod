Good to know

Questions.

Do you ship outside Johannesburg?

Yes — we ship nationwide across South Africa. Let us know your city or suburb in your DM and we'll come back with the delivery details.

Can I see a piece before I buy it?

Absolutely. Our showroom is at 66 Grant Avenue, Corner Nellie Road, Norwood. Send us a DM to arrange a viewing time.

How do I reserve or buy something?

Send @retromod.za a DM with the piece you're interested in. Stock moves quickly, so we confirm availability and arrange payment and delivery with you directly.

Do you buy furniture, art and audio?

Yes — we're buyers as well as sellers. Send us clear photos, the maker if you know it, and any history, and we'll be in touch.

Where do new pieces appear first?

On Instagram (@retromod.za) and in our WhatsApp community. The shop on this site lists what's currently on the floor.
